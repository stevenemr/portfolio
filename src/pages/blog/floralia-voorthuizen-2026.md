### Voting process

<img src="/blog/floralia-2026/P1020200.JPG" class="w-full md:w-1/2 md:float-end md:ml-10 rounded-lg">

It was awesome to see many people use the voting page, this was really a proud moment. At the event, I met some of the jury members. We spoke and I was asked to take part in the voting process to check if the scores were added to the database correctly. It was a good thing we checked 
first, because there was a server error. I could directly fix the error, so the voting went perfectly further on.

As I was part of it, the jury and I could already see some improvements for the next edition. It was insightful to see your application used by other people, because they have a different view of using the application and don't always see the *happy flow*. 

### Conclusion

Many thanks to Bart Nooteboom for inviting me to this amazing event, I really enjoyed it!
